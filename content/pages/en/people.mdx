Educational explanations draw on published versions of the knowledge base. Proposals, reviews and changes retain their history.

### Medical proposal

An authorized editor proposes content and source changes within their scope.



### Review independent of the author

Another authorized person reviews the proposal and may approve it or request changes.



### Controlled publication

Approval and publication are recorded separately. Health consults published content matching the context.

## Before entering Harbor Health.

These are the distinctions to understand before using the product.

### Does Harbor Health make a diagnosis?

No. It offers organization and educational explanations. It does not recommend treatment or replace individual assessment by a healthcare professional.



### Can I upload a scanned test report?

Yes. Scanned PDFs and images are supported. Document quality affects recognition; always review the extracted information.



### Does every document have an explanation?

Not necessarily. Extraction and explanation are different stages. An explanation requires a knowledge base approved for the relevant category, country and language.



### Can I use Harbor on my phone?

A responsive Web/PWA experience is available. It is not presented as an application distributed through mobile app stores.



### Does it connect automatically to my laboratory?

For now, upload documents or paste their text. Automatic laboratory connections remain pending.
